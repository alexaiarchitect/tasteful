Why a theme needs a preview

Contrast ratios and token names can be checked by a script, but rhythm and tone need eyes. This block uses every element the prose styles cover, including inline code.

Хорошая типографика незаметна: читатель видит смысл, а не шрифт.

La buena tipografía pasa desapercibida: el lector ve las ideas, no la letra.

What to look at

  • Heading weight against body text
  • Link colour on the background
  • Card lift on hover
  1. Open the light panel
  2. Compare with the dark panel
Typography exists to honour content.
@import "tailwindcss";
@source "../";

muted / bgSecondary text4.5:1
accent / bgLinks, focus3:1

Font stacks (no web fonts are loaded in the preview; the first installed family renders):

  • --font-sans: "Inter Variable", ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif
  • --font-display: "Source Serif 4 Variable", "Iowan Old Style", "Palatino Linotype", Georgia, serif
  • --font-mono: "JetBrains Mono Variable", ui-monospace, "SF Mono", Menlo, Consolas, monospace
