# Brief: DJI Osmo Pocket settings guide

A reference guide and settings picker for the DJI Osmo Pocket camera: choose a scenario (vlog, low light, sport) and get recommended resolution, frame rate, shutter, ISO and colour profile.
Audience: hobbyist creators who want the right settings fast, often on a phone while shooting.
Tone: technical, direct, trustworthy. Content density: dense — comparison tables, spec lists and short how-to steps.
Motion budget: 0 (no transitions beyond colour). Dark mode: both, since it is used outdoors and at night.
Must have: compact tables, a scannable nav for many sections, chips for settings values, and a strong spacing scale.
