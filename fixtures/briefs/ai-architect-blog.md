# Brief: AI architect blog + landing

A personal site for an independent AI-architecture consultant: a landing page that explains the offer and a long-form blog with technical essays.
The site is trilingual (English, Russian, Spanish), so long Russian and Spanish words must not break headings or cards, and every font needs Cyrillic.
Audience: CTOs and engineering leads evaluating a consultant; they read carefully and distrust hype.
Tone: calm, precise, warm. Content density: text-heavy — essays with code blocks, tables and diagrams.
Motion budget: 1 (subtle hover and fade only). Dark mode: both schemes, equal quality.
Must have: readable prose, a quiet top nav, an article card system, and a clear contact call to action.
