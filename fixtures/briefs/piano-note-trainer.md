# Brief: piano note-reading trainer

A small web tool that teaches beginners to read sheet music: it shows a note on a staff and the learner taps the matching piano key.
Audience: adult beginners and kids practising with a parent; sessions are short, a few minutes a day.
Tone: playful, calm, encouraging — friendly without being noisy or childish.
Content density: balanced — one focused exercise screen plus a few short explainer pages.
Motion budget: 1 (gentle feedback on right and wrong answers, nothing that distracts from the staff).
Dark mode: light-first, dark available. Must have: big clear buttons, status chips for right/wrong, and a simple progress card.
